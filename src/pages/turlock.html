Local questions

Turlock Questions, Answered

Natural or modern: which suits my home?

Natural ponds and streams suit yards with trees, lawn and room for boulders and planting. Modern water walls suit stucco homes, patios and smaller yards where a clean line works better. We can show you both in 3D before you choose.

What are Turlock's watering days?

From March 1 to October 31, even-numbered homes water on Tuesday and Saturday, and odd-numbered homes on Wednesday and Sunday. From November 1 to February 28, even-numbered homes water on Saturday and odd-numbered homes on Sunday. Watering is allowed before 9 a.m. and after 9 p.m., and not during or within 48 hours after measurable rain (City of Turlock).

Does a water feature use a lot of water?

No. A recirculating feature reuses the same water and loses a little to evaporation, which an automatic fill line tops up. Drip irrigation for the planting around it is exempt from Turlock's watering days unless the City declares Stage 4.

Will a pond bring mosquitoes?

Not if the water keeps moving and the pond has fish. The Turlock Mosquito Abatement District provides mosquitofish for ornamental ponds, usually from May to October. Pick them up on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., or ask the district to place them: (209) 634-1234 (district page).

Do I need a permit for a pond?

It depends on the depth and design. Deep ponds can need a permit and a safety barrier, while water walls and pondless features hold no deep open water. We check the City of Turlock's requirements for your project before we build.

What does a water feature cost?

Price depends on the size, the materials, access to your yard and what's built around the water. Design is priced by the size of your yard, and not every project needs one; the 3D is free. You get a written price after we see your yard.
