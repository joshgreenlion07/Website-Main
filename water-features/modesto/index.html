Pond or Pondless?

Both give you the sound of falling water. The difference is what's at the bottom of the falls.

Koi Pond

A pond you can sit beside, with fish, water plants and a waterfall feeding it.

  • Koi, goldfish and water lilies
  • Filtration sized for the fish
  • Open water, so plan around small children
  • Regular care: feeding, filters and seasonal cleaning

Pondless Waterfall or Stream

Water runs over the rocks and drops into a hidden basin under gravel, then the pump sends it back to the top.

  • The sound and movement, with no open pool
  • Fits small yards and side yards
  • Less upkeep than a pond
  • Can be turned off when you're away

Modesto Questions, Answered

Pond or pondless: which is right for my yard?

Choose a pond if you want fish and a place to sit by the water. Choose pondless if you want the sound and movement with no open pool, less upkeep or a smaller footprint. Not sure? We can show you both in 3D.

Are water features allowed under Modesto's water rules?

Yes. The City of Modesto allows ornamental fountains that use recirculated water (City of Modesto). Every Green Lion pond and pondless feature recirculates. Planting around it goes on drip, which the City allows at any time.

What are Modesto's watering days?

From November 1 to March 31, odd-numbered addresses water on Wednesday and Sunday, and even-numbered addresses on Tuesday and Saturday. There's no watering from noon to 7 p.m., and none on Monday, Thursday or Friday. The schedule changes in spring, so check the City's watering schedule.

Can I get a rebate for replacing the lawn around the feature?

The City of Modesto's Turf Replacement Program pays $2 per square foot, up to $6,000 per home each fiscal year, for drought-tolerant plants or artificial turf products that replace grass. It covers products, not labor. You apply and pass a pre-inspection before any work starts, and sprinklers in the area are capped or converted to drip. Funding is limited (City of Modesto).

Does Modesto's clay soil matter for a pond?

Yes. Soils around Modesto range from sandy loam to heavy clay, and some yards hit a hard layer a few feet down. We check the soil when we dig and plan the depth, drainage and base around it.

Will a pond bring mosquitoes?

Not if the water keeps moving and the pond has fish. Mosquito abatement districts around Modesto take mosquitofish requests for ornamental ponds, including the East Side Mosquito Abatement District and the Turlock Mosquito Abatement District.

How do koi and pond plants handle a Modesto winter?

Valley winters are mild, with a few frost nights in the high 20s. Koi slow down and eat less in cold water, so feeding drops off in winter. Tender plants near the pond, such as elephant ears and citrus, need a frost cover on the coldest nights; our frost check shows which ones.

What does a pond or pondless waterfall cost?

Price depends on the size, the boulders and materials, access to your yard and what's built around the water. Design is priced by the size of your yard, and not every project needs one; the 3D is free. You get a written price after we see your yard.
