Local questions

Tracy Questions, Answered

Will the afternoon wind blow water off a water wall?

Tracy gets strong afternoon wind off the Altamont Pass, especially in summer. Wind pulls water off tall, thin sheets and spray-heavy spouts. A lower drop, a basin wide enough to catch the spill and an automatic fill line keep a water wall running full. We look at where the wind comes from before we place the wall.

Do I need HOA approval for a water feature?

Many Tracy-area neighborhoods have design rules. In Mountain House, the Master Restrictions include architectural controls with design standards and approval steps for changes to homes and landscaping (City of Mountain House). Check your HOA or CC&Rs before you build. A scaled 2D plan and 3D views make that submission easier.

Does a water feature waste water?

A recirculating water feature reuses the same water. It loses some to evaporation and wind, and an automatic fill line tops it up. We go over the City of Tracy's current water rules with you at the consultation, and the planting around the feature goes on drip.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the feature. Deep ponds can need a permit and a safety barrier, while most water walls and pondless features hold no deep open water. We check the City of Tracy's requirements for your project before we build.

Will a water feature bring mosquitoes?

Moving water doesn't give mosquitoes the still water they breed in, and a pump that runs keeps the surface moving. For ponds, the San Joaquin County Mosquito and Vector Control District has offered free delivery of mosquitofish for water features and ornamental ponds anywhere in the county (district notice). Call (209) 982-4675 to ask.

Which plants suit a modern water wall in Tracy's heat?

Plants that like full sun and little water keep the lines clean: agave, Mexican feather grass, blue fescue and yucca, with citrus in large pots. Agave needs dry winter soil, and citrus needs a frost cover on the coldest nights. Our frost check shows what to cover.

What does a modern water feature cost?

Price depends on the size, the materials, access to your yard and what's built around the water. Design is priced by the size of your yard, and not every project needs one; the 3D is free. You get a written price after we see your yard.
