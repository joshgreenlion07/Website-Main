This winter's plants, and how to take care of them

Central Valley winters are mild most days, then a clear, still night drops into the high 20s. Those few frost nights do most of the damage. The plants we install most often handle winter well when you know which ones need a cover, which ones need less water, and which ones just need a trim in February.

Use the frost check below the next time the forecast calls for a cold night.

Try it

Frost check: what needs a cover tonight?

31°FFrost likely
18°F32°F freeze45°F

    Cover with frost cloth that reaches the ground, not plastic touching the leaves. Water dry soil the day before. Moist soil holds more heat. Pull covers off in the morning.
